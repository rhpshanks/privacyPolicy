Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026 · Applies to every mobile game published by Hashaam Shahid Yousafzai (“the games”)

In one sentence: The games collect no personal information, send nothing over the internet, and show no advertising. They work completely offline, and the only thing they remember is your game progress, which stays on your device.

Who this policy covers

This one policy covers all of the games listed under this developer on app stores, including, among others, Sort Squad and Arrow Out. Each game links here. They are built the same way and handle data the same way, so a single policy describes them all. No game needs an account or sign in.

Information the games collect

None. The games do not collect, store or transmit personal information. Specifically, they do not gather any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses
  • Photos, camera images, microphone audio or voice recordings
  • Location of any kind, precise or approximate
  • Contacts, calendar entries, messages, call logs or files on the device
  • Advertising identifiers, device identifiers or any other persistent identifier
  • Usage analytics, event logs, crash reports or performance data

Information stored on your device

A game may save a small amount of progress so you do not have to start again every time:

What is savedWhyWhere it goes
Game progress, such as the highest level reached, and simple settings So you can carry on where you left off Stored locally on the device. It is never sent anywhere by the game.

This contains no personal information. It is typically a number such as 12. Uninstalling the game deletes it. If your device's own backup is switched on, the operating system may include it in that backup, which is handled by Google or Apple under their terms rather than by the game.

No internet, no advertising, no third parties

  • The games make no network requests while you play.
  • There is no advertising of any kind, and no advertising software is built in.
  • There are no in-app purchases and no payment handling.
  • There is no analytics, crash reporting or performance reporting software built in.
  • No data is shared with, or sold to, anybody. There is nothing to share.
  • The only link out of a game is the link to this policy, which opens in your web browser.

Permissions

The games ask for no sensitive device permissions. They do not request the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, phone or any similar permission.

Children's privacy

Some of the games are made for children. All of the games are designed to comply with children's privacy rules, including the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and, in Europe and the United Kingdom, the rules protecting children's data under the GDPR and UK GDPR.

Because the games collect no personal information at all, there is nothing to obtain parental consent for, nothing profiled, nothing used for targeted advertising, and nothing to request access to or deletion of. To remove saved progress, clear the game's data in your device settings, or uninstall the game.

Your rights

Data protection law gives people rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about them. The games hold no personal data about anybody, so there is nothing held to release, correct or erase. You can still send any question to the address below.

Changes to this policy

If a game ever changes what it does with data, for example by adding advertising or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date at the top will change. Any change affecting a particular game will name that game.
