Privacy Policy for Sort Squad

Effective 30 August 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.hashaamshahid.sortsquad

In one sentence: Sort Squad collects nothing, sends nothing, and shows no advertising. It works completely offline, and the only thing it remembers is the highest level reached, which stays on the device.

Who this app is for

Sort Squad is a sorting and matching game made for children, roughly ages 6 to 8. It was built so that a child can play it without any account, any sign in, and any way to reach the wider internet.

Information the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store or transmit personal information. Specifically, it does not gather any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses
  • Photos, camera images, microphone audio or voice recordings
  • Location of any kind, precise or approximate
  • Contacts, calendar entries, messages, call logs or files on the device
  • Advertising identifiers, device identifiers or any other persistent identifier
  • Usage analytics, event logs, crash reports or performance data

Information stored on the device

The app saves one value so a child does not have to start again every time:

What is savedWhyWhere it goes
The highest level number reached, saved as a single whole number So the title screen can offer to carry on rather than restart Stored locally by Android on the device. It is never sent anywhere.

This value contains no personal information. It is a number such as 3. Removing the app from the device deletes it. If the device owner has Android backup switched on, Android may include this value in the device's own backup, which is handled by Google under Google's terms rather than by this app.

No internet, no advertising, no third parties

  • The app contains no networking code and makes no network requests.
  • There is no advertising of any kind, and no advertising software is built in.
  • There are no in-app purchases and no payment handling.
  • There is no analytics, crash reporting or performance reporting software built in. The engine's own analytics, crash reporting and performance reporting services are switched off in this project.
  • No data is shared with, or sold to, anybody. There is nothing to share.
  • There are no social media links, external links or embedded web pages inside the game.

Permissions

The app asks for no sensitive device permissions. It does not request the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, phone or any similar permission.

Children's privacy

This app is directed at children and is designed to comply with children's privacy rules, including the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and, in Europe and the United Kingdom, the rules protecting children's data under the GDPR and UK GDPR.

Because the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing to obtain parental consent for, nothing profiled, nothing used for targeted advertising, and nothing to request access to or deletion of. If you would still like to remove the one saved value, you can clear the app's data in Android settings, or remove the app.

Your rights

Data protection law gives people rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about them. This app holds no personal data about anybody, so there is nothing held to release, correct or erase. Any question about this can still be sent to the address below.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it does with data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date at the top will change. Material changes affecting children's data would be described plainly here.
