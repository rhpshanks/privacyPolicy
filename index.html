Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · Applies to every mobile game published by Hashaam Shahid Yousafzai (“the games”)

Games without ads (Sort Squad) collect no personal information, send nothing over the internet, and show no advertising. They work completely offline, and the only thing they remember is your game progress, which stays on your device.

Games with ads (Cannon Smash, Arrow Out) show advertising from Google AdMob. To show and measure those ads, Google collects device information, including your device's advertising ID. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time. We, the developer, do not collect or receive any personal information ourselves.

None, in any game. The games themselves do not collect, store or transmit personal information. We do not gather any of the following, and we have no server that could receive it:

The one exception is the advertising in games that show ads. That data is collected by Google, not by us, and is described in the next section.

Advertising (Cannon Smash and Arrow Out)

Who provides the ads

Cannon Smash and Arrow Out show ads through Google AdMob, the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google LLC (and by Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland). The ads are:

  • Banner ads along the bottom of the screen during play.
  • Interstitial ads, full-screen ads that may appear between levels, no more than once every few levels.
  • Rewarded ads, which only ever play when you choose to watch one in return for a reward, such as extra balls or a power-up in Cannon Smash, or an extra heart or more hints in Arrow Out. They are always optional.
  • App open ads (Arrow Out only), a full-screen ad that may appear when you open the game or come back to it after being away.

Ads are requested with a maximum content rating of PG. The games have no in-app purchases.

What Google collects

When the game requests and shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google information such as:

  • Device identifiers: your device's advertising ID (on Android, the Google advertising ID) and the app set ID.
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address. The game never asks for precise GPS location.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system, language, screen size, network type, and the app's name and version.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, viewed or tapped.
  • Diagnostics: information about how the ad SDK itself performed, including crash and error data.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, to measure how ads perform, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you have allowed it, to personalise ads to your interests. Google describes this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, in the Google Privacy Policy, and in its AdMob data disclosure. Google keeps and deletes this data under its own policies.

We do not see or receive this information. AdMob only gives us totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned, which do not identify anyone.

Your choices and consent

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the first time you open a game with ads, a consent form from Google asks whether ads may use your data, including for personalisation. No ads are requested until the form has been answered. You can change your answer at any time: in Cannon Smash from Settings → Privacy Options, and in Arrow Out from the Ad choices link on the game screen.
  • Everywhere: you can stop personalised ads, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in your device settings. On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • United States: depending on your state, you may have the right to opt out of the use of your data for targeted advertising. Deleting or resetting your advertising ID as described above does this. We do not sell personal information for money.
  • You can also play these games without an internet connection, in which case no ads load and no data is sent.

Internet, purchases and third parties

  • Games without ads make no network requests at all while you play.
  • Games with ads connect to the internet only through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, to load and show ads and to show the consent form.
  • There are no in-app purchases and no payment handling in any game.
  • There is no analytics, crash reporting or performance reporting software of our own in any game.
  • We share no data with anybody. In games with ads, the only third party that receives data is Google, as the ad provider, as described above.
  • The links out of a game are the link to this policy and, in games with ads, any ad you choose to tap. Both open outside the game.

Permissions

The games ask for no sensitive device permissions. They do not request the camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, phone or any similar permission.

Games with ads also include the ordinary permissions the ad SDK needs, none of which shows a prompt: internet access, network state, and the Android advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID). Games that vibrate include the vibration permission, used only for short taps of feedback during play.

Children's privacy

Games without ads collect no personal information at all, so they are suitable for every age. Nothing is profiled, used for targeted advertising, or held that could need a parent's consent.

Cannon Smash and Arrow Out are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Their ads are limited to a PG content rating. If we ever list a game with ads as being for children, its ads will be requested as child-directed, which turns off personalised advertising, in line with the United States Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and UK GDPR, and Google Play's Families Policy. If you believe a child has used a game with ads and want the ad data removed, delete or reset the device's advertising ID as described above, and contact us.

Your rights

Data protection law, including the GDPR, UK GDPR and United States state laws, gives people rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data held about them, and to object to its use. We hold no personal data about anybody, so there is nothing for us to release, correct or erase. For data Google collects through ads in games with ads, you can use the in-game privacy options, your device's ad settings, and the tools Google provides at My Ad Center. You can still send any question to the address below.
